Frequently asked questions

Is Jobless free for job seekers?

Yes. Searching, saving and applying never cost anything.

How do I post a job?

Open Post a Job, fill in the form and check the live preview. Your listing appears on Discover right away.

Are these real listings?

Yes. Listings are pulled from Remotive and refreshed every few hours, and Apply takes you to the original posting. Jobless is a practice project, though, so jobs you post here, and the contact and sign-up forms, stay in demo mode.

Where is my data stored?

Saved jobs, jobs you post and your theme choice stay in your browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
